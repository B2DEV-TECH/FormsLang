# 13. Enterprise usage

For a client engagement, copy or mount authorized source read-only where practical, create a named project and record source coverage before interpreting recommendations. Keep customer code out of public issue reports.

Assign specialists operationally: Forms experts inspect legacy behavior, APEX developers review native mappings, architects resolve cross-layer ownership/security, and business owners decide ambiguous intent. Team assignment and centralized collaboration are not new 2.0 services.

Use reports and the manifest as a milestone handoff. Record unresolved decisions instead of forcing answers. A completed assessment can be useful while generation remains blocked. Review rationale and history belong with the exact source/analysis revision.

The CLI can produce headless assessments and reports in your existing pipeline. Retain outputs under your own access policy. CSV/JSON support backlog transfer without requiring Jira/Azure connectors.

Scale evidence is synthetic, not a customer SLA or analyst-hours claim. Large estates can take seconds to load/read; inspect [acceptance measurements](../quality-acceptance.md) before planning throughput. Future portfolio/team improvements must not weaken local-first controls.
